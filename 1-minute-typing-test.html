Why One Minute Typing Tests Work

  • Quick measurement of typing speed
  • Easy to repeat multiple times
  • Great for daily practice routines
  • Helps improve focus and typing rhythm

Tips to Improve Your Typing Speed

  • Focus on accuracy before speed
  • Keep your fingers on the home row keys
  • Look at the screen instead of the keyboard
  • Practice consistently for 10–15 minutes per day
